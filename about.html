OUR STORY

From Sun Engineers to Sun Automations

Our journey began in 1991, with the establishment of Sun Engineers, built on a simple belief that quality engineering and reliable machines could make a meaningful difference to industry.For more than three decades, we have grown alongside the changing needs of manufacturing. As technology advanced and industries embraced automation, we continued to learn, adapt and innovate.

This evolution led to the transformation of our engineering capabilities into Sun Automations, a new chapter focused on modern automation, smarter machines and innovative industrial solutions. Today, Sun Engineers and Sun Automations represent the journey from traditional engineering to technology-driven manufacturing. While our capabilities have evolved, our foundation remains the same: quality, reliability, innovation and a commitment to helping our customers build a stronger future.
